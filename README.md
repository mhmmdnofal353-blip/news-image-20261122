## The day's stories

- **Lead — COP31:** UN climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, end on Sunday
  with no agreement — the first COP to fail to adopt a deal since
  Copenhagen in 2009 — after the EU, Panama, Norway and island states
  reject a final text that never once mentions fossil fuels. The summit
  ran two days into overtime and became the longest COP on record.
- **Gulf — Iran war:** Iran strikes Bahrain, and Houthi missiles are
  fired at Saudi Arabia early on Sunday. Bahrain says an Iranian attack
  set a civilian industrial site ablaze; the Revolutionary Guards say
  they targeted US military positions in the kingdom, home to the US
  Navy's Fifth Fleet. Gulf stock markets fall.
- **Indonesia:** A fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in
  Cikande, Serang, Banten, kills three workers and leaves 36 missing;
  rescuers search the burned plant on Sunday while 15 workers are
  pulled out alive.
- **Nepal — India:** Nepal's prime minister Balendra Shah begins a
  two-day state visit to India, meeting Narendra Modi in New Delhi.
- **Also today:** India declare 720/9 against New Zealand in
  Wellington; GTA VI sets a $3.79bn launch-day record; Trump seeks a
  "permanent" deal to block Iran's nuclear programme; a Houthi missile
  is intercepted over Saudi Arabia.

## How it was made

The front page was composed programmatically (Python + Pillow) from four
photo-realistic editorial illustrations generated for this entry, then
saved as a 1024×1024 JPEG (`entry.png`, 279,468 bytes — under the 1 MiB
limit). The program in this repository, `main.ts`, simply copies that
image to the output directory, verifying its magic bytes and that the
copy is byte-identical.
